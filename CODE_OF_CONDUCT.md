# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in the NOTWEB community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We welcome contributors regardless of experience level, background, identity, nationality, or personal characteristics.

Our goal is to build an open-source community where people can collaborate, learn, share ideas, and improve software together.

## Expected Behavior

All community members are expected to:

- Be respectful, professional, and considerate.
- Welcome constructive feedback and different viewpoints.
- Focus on ideas, code, and technical decisions rather than personal attacks.
- Give credit to other people's work and contributions.
- Accept reasonable criticism and suggestions.
- Help newcomers understand the project.
- Communicate clearly and respectfully.
- Respect the privacy and security of other contributors.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or hateful language.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or development.
- Trolling, insulting, or repeatedly provoking community members.
- Misrepresenting another person's work.
- Sharing malicious code, credentials, or private data.
- Any other conduct that creates an unsafe or hostile environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues and pull requests.
- Code reviews and project discussions.
- Official community communication channels.
- Events or activities representing NOTWEB.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainers through an available private communication channel.

Please include relevant context and evidence when safe and appropriate.

Do not publish sensitive personal information or confidential security details in public issues.

Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and fairly.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

Depending on the severity and circumstances, responses may include:

1. A request to stop the behavior.
2. A warning.
3. Temporary restrictions on participation.
4. Removal from project spaces or other appropriate actions.

Actions should be proportionate to the behavior and applied consistently.

## Fairness and Privacy

Reports will be reviewed as objectively as possible.

Information about a report should only be shared with people who need it to address the issue, subject to applicable safety and legal requirements.

Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is unacceptable.

## Acknowledgment

By participating in the NOTWEB community, you agree to follow these guidelines and help maintain a constructive, inclusive environment.

Thank you for helping make NOTWEB a better place to build software.
